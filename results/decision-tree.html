Declare Concept-Drift — Detection → Confirmation → Labeling

Threshold-free, standard-Declare lenses, incremental batch pipeline. Recall-first detection feeds the decision tree using ALL change points as window delimiters; a drift is OUTPUT when its intensity ≥ 1% of tracked relations OR the tree gives it a structural label (dual gate). Metrics on Ostovar (~3000-trace, 2 drifts/log) and Ceravolo-1000. K=0.999 two-proportion z-test (support ∧ confidence), Kborn=0.99, batch≈100, lag=200.

A detected change point does double duty: it delimits the labeling windows and is a candidate drift. So all CPs (weak + strong) are kept for windowing — that preserves the fine-grained regimes the signature needs — while the dual gate decides what to output. The dual gate (intensity ≥ 1% OR structural label) lifts Ostovar detection F1 0.44 → 0.80 and keeps end-to-end labeling at its full level (48/114) — it rescues subtle low-intensity drifts (branch/skip) via their own label, where an intensity-only cut drops one (48→47). Ceravolo never engages the filter (its true-drift intensities all exceed 1%).

They look harsh on the occurrence leaves because composites are superpositions routed to one dominant operation (a composite's insert-component is a false-positive on INSERTION and a miss for COMPOSITE). The operation-level precision (last column) — did the drift genuinely perform the identified operation? — is ≈1.0 for every structural leaf. Ceravolo has no clean atomic insertion/removal/substitution label (its re/rp present as duplication/insertion), so those leaves have no true members there.
